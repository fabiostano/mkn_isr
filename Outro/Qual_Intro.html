In the next part of the study, you will see four pages with open questions. The questions ask you to reflect on your experiences during the tasks and to compare your experiences in the chat and video meeting settings.
Please take your time and answer the questions carefully and thoughtfully.

Our study is about flow experiences in team interactions. The flow-experience has been described by statements like these:

‘My mind isn't wandering. I am totally involved in what I am doing, and I am not thinking of anything else. My body feels good. The world seems to be cut off from me. I am less aware of myself and my problems.’
‘My concentration is like breathing. I never think of it. When I start to experience flow, I really do shut out the world.’
‘When I am in the flow, I am so involved in what I am doing. I don't see myself as separate from what I am doing.

On the next pages, we will ask about: (1) your own flow experience during the tasks, and
(2) how interaction with your team member(s) may have influenced your flow experience(s).
