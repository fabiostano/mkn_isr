Block 3: Headphone-EEG Recording in a Virtual Meeting

{{ endblock }} {{ block content }}

This block takes about 30 minutes and should be completed at your scheduled time slot (communicated to you before the study). Please find a place where you feel comfortable having a virtual meeting.

It has two stages:

  1. Rest & mental arithmetic (~5 min) – a short warm-up task at the start
  2. Team meeting (~25 min) – you'll join a virtual meeting with your team and complete a series of team mental arithmetic tasks. Please allow camera and microphone access when prompted.

Before you begin, please set up the EEG recording with your headphones (see the next 2 pages for instructions).
