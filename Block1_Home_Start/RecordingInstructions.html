Block 1: Headphone-EEG Recording at Home

{{ endblock }} {{ block content }}

This block takes about 1 hour and should be completed at home, in your typical home-office environment.

It has three stages:

  1. Rest & mental arithmetic (~5 min) – a short warm-up task at the start
  2. Free work session (~45 min) – work on whatever you like (no interruptions)
  3. Rest & mental arithmetic (~5 min) – the same short task again, at the end

Please plan to stay at your PC for the entire hour, ideally without bathroom breaks or other interruptions, and make sure you have a stable internet connection throughout.

Before you begin, please set up the EEG recording with your headphones (see the next 2 pages for instructions).
