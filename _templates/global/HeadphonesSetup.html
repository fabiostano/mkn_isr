Now we need to prepare an EEG recording with the headphones.

Preparation

Before putting on the headphones, please:

  1. Remove chewing gum or similar food.
  2. Remove any earrings that would touch the cushions
  3. Leave your hair open (i.e. not in a bun or similar)
  4. Take off your glasses for now (you can put them on after you put on the headphones)

Finally, please use the alcohol pad on your table to clean the skin around your ear thorougly (repeat at least 2x!).

Putting on the Headphones

Now, to put on the headphones:

  1. Rotate the headphones the right way (the left side has the black band)
  2. Put the headphones on so that they sit comfortably
  3. Move hair out of the way of the electrodes
  4. Adjust the headband fit and wiggle the electrodes
  5. Also push on the side for 3 seconds to make sure the electrodes have good skin contact
  6. If you are wearing glasses, you can put them on now. Make sure they sit above the the earcups.

Great, you are now ready to start a recording!
You will find instructions for that after a few questions.
