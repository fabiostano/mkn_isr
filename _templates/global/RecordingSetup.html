Signal Quality Check

Now we need to start a recording with the EEG headphones.

To do that, the most important goal is to ensure that all electrodes have good skin contact. In the upcoming recording tool, you can see if an electrode has good contact (green) or bad contact (red).

Your goal in this next stage is to make sure that there are no "red" electrodes. Please beware, that this is often not achieved on the first try. If an electrode is bad (red), please try to move hair out of the way and press on the electrode for a few seconds. Sometimes it also really helps to just wait for 5-10 seconds.

If you have tried for some time, but an electrode just won't improve, please proceed with the experiment anyways.

The Recording Tool

To check the signals and start a recording, we have developed the following recording tool.

  1. Follow the on-screen instructions to connect to the headset.
  2. After selecting the port that says "gekoppelt", the signal quality check starts automatically.
  3. When all channels are green (or don't improve further), click on "Start recording". You will be asked to complete two final checks. Please follow the instructions accordingly.
  4. When the impedance check is completed, click on "Continue" - then the recording is started. You should then return to this experiment tab. Leave the recording tool website tab open (running in the background).
